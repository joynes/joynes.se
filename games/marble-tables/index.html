Marble Tables

Pick up a marble and drop it over the ramps. New marbles fall in automatically. Experiment without scores or time limits.

Select Start. The game downloads about 15 MB.

Touch or mouse: hold a marble, move it and release. Keyboard: focus the playground, move the crosshair with arrow keys, and hold Space or Enter to grab and drag. Shift gives fine control. Escape releases the marble; Home centres the crosshair. Landscape works best on small screens.

About the web version

The new web interface was made using AI. The original 2022 Unity engine, 3D scene and physics are preserved. Progress is not saved. Older Unity WebGL builds may behave differently across phones.
